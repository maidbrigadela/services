Here's What You Need to Know

Communication Is Key

We love to exceed your expectations, but first we need to know them! Communicate your expectations clearly to our office staff so that we may best meet your needs. If you have a particular task or objective, let us know! We're happy to help.

Our Products

We arrive at your home fully equipped and ready to go! We use electrolyzed water cleaning solutions and disinfectants; we do not use chemicals such as acids, alkalis, or bleaches, which means zero harsh chemicals or fumes. Maid Brigade's Exclusive PUREcleaning® System uses allergy-free and anti-bacterial color-coded microfiber cloths and mops which are non-abrasive and eliminate cross-contamination. Our HEPA-filtration vacuums are also certified through the Carpet and Rug Institute's Green Label program.

Our Teams

Our cleaning teams are employees with competitive pay and benefits, not independent contractors. This means we can guarantee every person we send into your home for integrity and quality. It also means our staff is fully insured, including being covered by Worker's Compensation insurance, so there is zero risk or liability on your end.

Note: PUREmist® disinfection is available upon request and is not automatically included in every service. Please let us know ahead of time if you would like it added.

How to Prepare for Your Appointment

Cancellation / Reschedule

If you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment for any reason, please call or email us at least 48 business hours prior to your scheduled cleaning date to avoid the late cancellation fee equivalent to the cost of service. Our office hours are 7:30 AM - 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday. If it is after hours, leave a message and we will get back to you on the next business day. If we are unable to gain access to your home on the day of service, a "Lock Out" fee equivalent to your cleaning fee will be charged.

De-Clutter

You know where things go in your home better than we do! Our teams are best when focusing on cleaning tasks, so it's best that you handle clutter and putting things away. It's not our intention that you "clean" before we get to your home, but we do ask that general clutter, clothing, toys, etc. be picked up as much as possible as this preparation allows us to focus on cleaning surfaces and not spending time tidying.

Pets

If you have any pets, please be sure to let us know so we can make our teams aware. If you have dogs, they must be securely put away or outside while we service your home. The safety of our employees and your pet(s) is our priority! Note: We do not clean any pet waste.

Arrival Times

We will arrive between 8:00 AM and 5:00 PM. To get a two-hour window of arrival, you can call or email our office as early as 7:30 AM on the morning of your service and we can provide that narrowed estimated arrival window then. Please remember that your team could arrive as early as 8:00 AM.

Entry

The easiest, most popular method is utilizing our Key Service. We'll keep your key coded and secure between visits. If you'd like to take advantage of the Key Service, please let us know and we can coordinate getting your house key to the office for safekeeping. You can also provide a garage code or hide-a-key location.
