PUREcleaning® FAQs

What is PUREcleaning®?

PUREcleaning is Maid Brigade's proprietary cleaning system, combining electrolyzed water solutions, color-coded tools, hospital-grade disinfection, and HEPA filtration to clean and sanitize your home more thoroughly without the harsh chemicals found in many traditional cleaning products.

What is electrolyzed water, and is it safe?

Electrolyzed water solutions ("e-water") are created using only water, salt, and electricity. The process produces sodium hydroxide, which powers our multi-surface cleaner, and hypochlorous acid, the same compound your own immune system produces to fight germs. Both are safe and effective without the synthetic fragrances, dyes, or heavy residue of many traditional products.

How is PUREcleaning different from a standard cleaning?

A standard clean focuses on visible dirt and dust. PUREcleaning adds a system for stopping germs from spreading while we clean: color-coded towels prevent cross-contamination between rooms, and every surface is cleaned in the same top-to-bottom, left-to-right order so nothing gets missed or re-contaminated.

What do the color-coded towels mean?

Every towel we use is assigned to a specific type of surface or room, so a towel used in your bathroom never touches your kitchen counters, and vice versa.

What makes your vacuums different?

Our backpack vacuums use 4-tier HEPA filtration, capturing 99.9% of dust, allergens, pollen, and other irritants, including particles in the air too small to see. That means a deeper clean for surfaces and better air quality after we leave.

What's the difference between PUREcleaning and PUREmist?

PUREcleaning is the full cleaning and sanitizing system used on every visit. PUREmist is an optional add-on that uses an electrostatic sprayer to apply our disinfectant to high-touch surfaces for 360-degree coverage.

What is barrier protection / Bioprotect500?

Barrier protection is an optional add-on applied right after a PUREmist treatment. It uses Bioprotect500, a microbiostatic agent that creates a protective barrier on treated surfaces, continuing to kill germs on them for up to 3 months.

Is PUREcleaning safe for kids and pets?

Our electrolyzed water solutions are formulated without the synthetic fragrances and harsh chemical residue found in many traditional cleaners. They are 100% safe for kids, pets, and food prep areas.

Is PUREcleaning included on every visit?

Yes! PUREcleaning is standard with every service. PUREmist disinfection and barrier protection are optional add-on services.
