PUREcleaning® is the Healthier Way to Clean

Do you really know what’s in the cleaning products being used in your home?

Most traditional cleaners are loaded with harsh chemicals, volatile organic compounds (VOCs), artificial fragrances, and toxic residues that can linger in the air and on surfaces long after the job is done. VOCs are gases emitted from many common household products that can irritate eyes, nose, and throat, cause headaches, and contribute to long-term health concerns, especially for kids, pets, and anyone with allergies or sensitivities.

At Maid Brigade, we do things differently. Our exclusive PUREcleaning® system uses powerful, non-toxic, hospital-grade solutions that actually protect your home and your loved ones.

Electrolyzed Water (e-water) – Nature’s Powerful Cleaner

We use electrolyzed water, made from just salt, water, and electricity, to create two highly effective, food-safe cleaning agents:

  • Sodium Hydroxide: Cuts through grease, grime, and built-up residue from previous cleaners without harsh fumes or damage to surfaces.
  • Hypochlorous Acid: A gentle yet powerful disinfectant that’s naturally produced by your own body’s immune system. It kills bacteria, viruses, and pathogens effectively while remaining completely safe for kids, pets, and plants.

We even use a specialized e-water glass cleaner that leaves surfaces streak-free and residue-free.

PUREmist® Electrostatic Disinfection*

For an extra layer of protection (ideal for families with young children, elderly members, or frequent guests), we offer electrostatic sprayer disinfection using our hypochlorous acid solution.

  • 100% coverage even on curved and hard-to-reach surfaces
  • Food-safe and non-toxic
  • Kills mold & mildew, common cold & flu viruses, Salmonella, E. coli, COVID-19, and many more pathogens
  • Proper dwell time without the mess of spraying, waiting 10 minutes, and wiping (which often re-contaminates the surface)

This gives you true hospital-grade disinfection that’s safe for your entire household.

Color-Coded Tools For ZERO Cross-Contamination

Unlike other independent cleaners or companies who often use the same towel from your bathroom straight to your kitchen, our strict color-coded system completely eliminates cross-contamination and the spread of germs between areas.

We never guess. Every tool has a dedicated purpose:

  • Red – Bathrooms & toilets
  • Yellow – Kitchen & food-prep areas
  • Green – Dusting & general surfaces
  • Blue – Glass, mirrors & windows

Even our mops are color-coded. This system completely eliminates the risk of spreading germs from one area of your home to another.

Our commercial-grade backpack vacuums feature 4-tier HEPA filtration that captures 99.9% of dust, allergens, pollen, and microscopic particles, including the ones you can’t see floating in the air. This is especially important for families with asthma, allergies, or pets.
