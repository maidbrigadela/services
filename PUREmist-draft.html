Understanding PUREmist® Disinfection

How It Works, Safety, and Environmental Impact

PUREmist® is Maid Brigade's optional electrostatic disinfection service, an added layer of protection after your home is cleaned with our PUREcleaning® system.

Once visible dust, dirt, and allergens are removed, we apply our hypochlorous acid disinfectant with a handheld electrostatic sprayer. The charged mist wraps around curved and flat surfaces for 360-degree coverage, reaching corners and crevices a cloth can't. Because it doesn't need to be wiped away, the disinfectant stays on surfaces for the full contact time it needs, with no wiping that can re-contaminate them.

About PUREmist®

PUREmist® uses a solution created from just three ingredients: water, salt, and electricity. Through a process called electrolysis, these elements form hypochlorous acid, a disinfecting agent that's effective against pathogens while remaining gentle for everyday environments. Our solution:

  • Is EPA-registered as a disinfectant
  • Contains no added dyes or synthetic fragrances
  • Leaves minimal residue
  • Is safe for use around children and pets when applied properly

We focus on high-touch areas like door handles, light switches, drawer pulls, and more. Because it's applied electrostatically, the mist clings to surfaces for 360-degree coverage, including crevices that traditional spray-and-wipe methods may miss.

A Healthier Home for Your Family

Complete Disinfection in Minutes

PUREmist® kills germs, bacteria, and viruses within minutes of application. Our electrostatic sprayer applies hypochlorous acid evenly across high-touch surfaces throughout your home. Hypochlorous acid is the same compound your body's own immune system produces to fight germs, so it's powerful against pathogens while remaining safe for kids, pets, and food-prep areas.

It's effective against common cold and flu viruses, COVID-19, Salmonella, E. coli, mold, and mildew. Ideal for families with young children, older relatives, or frequent guests, and especially during cold and flu season.

PUREmist® FAQs

What is PUREmist®?

PUREmist is an optional disinfection service that applies our EPA-registered hypochlorous acid disinfectant with an electrostatic sprayer. It's added after your home is cleaned with our PUREcleaning system.

How is PUREmist different from regular cleaning?

Cleaning removes dirt, grime, and residue. PUREmist disinfects, targeting germs on high-touch surfaces. The electrostatic mist coats surfaces evenly and stays on them long enough to work, without being wiped away.

Is hypochlorous acid safe for my family and pets?

Yes. Hypochlorous acid is the same compound your body's immune system makes to fight germs. Our solution contains no dyes or synthetic fragrances and is safe around kids, pets, and food-prep areas when applied properly.

What does PUREmist protect against?

It's effective against common cold and flu viruses, COVID-19, Salmonella, E. coli, mold, mildew, and many other pathogens.

Why does it smell a little like chlorine?

Hypochlorous acid can have a faint scent similar to chlorine. It contains no bleach, and the scent fades quickly after application.

Do I need to wipe surfaces or leave the house afterward?

No wiping is needed. The mist dries on its own, and you don't need to leave your home during or after treatment. Confirm re-entry guidance

How often should I schedule PUREmist?

Many families add it during cold and flu season, after someone has been sick, or before hosting guests. It can be added to any regular cleaning.

Can I add barrier protection after PUREmist?

Yes. Barrier protection with Bioprotect500 is applied right after a PUREmist treatment and forms a protective barrier on treated surfaces, continuing to kill germs on them for up to 3 months.
